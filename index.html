What's a Mystery Scoop?

A little surprise, wrapped just for you

A Mystery Scoop is a curated surprise box filled with a mix of charms, accessories, skincare accessories, and mugs. You won't know exactly what's inside — that's the fun part — but you can pick one or several categories you love (mix makeup, accessories and skincare together, for example), and even guarantee a specific item or two from our shop inside your box. Everything else stays a surprise, built just around your picks.

Prefer to know exactly what you're getting? No worries — all our individual products below can be purchased separately too.

Our Story

"Touta's World started as a small dream — the kind you hold onto quietly, hoping one day it becomes real. It began with a simple belief: that happiness doesn't have to be big or expensive. Sometimes it's hidden in small things — a charm, a color, a surprise you didn't see coming.

That's where the idea of the Mystery Scoop was born. Life itself feels like a mystery scoop sometimes — you never really know what's inside until you open it. So why not make that feeling fun instead of scary?

Every product here is chosen with care, the same way you'd pick a gift for someone you love. Touta's World isn't just a store — it's a little reminder that good things often come in small, unexpected packages. And maybe, just maybe, you're always just one scoop away from happiness."
